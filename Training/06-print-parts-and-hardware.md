# 06 · Print parts and hardware

The workspace links to two existing parts folders rather than duplicating large mesh and printer files:

- **SO-101 Print Parts** contains original/sectioned Prusa projects, print G-code, leader and follower STLs, and `Camera_Module_3_Mount/` source, mesh, license, and upstream notes.
- **Follower Print Parts** contains 12 extracted STL mesh shells and `README.txt` with approximate dimensions and triangle counts. They are split meshes, not an assembly guide; use the source model and hardware instructions to identify fit and fasteners.

The printed structure and mounting affect repeatability. A loose base, flexible mount, cable snag, or changed camera pose alters the observations/actions and can make demonstrations inconsistent. Inspect fasteners and cable routing before each session. A camera mounted to the wrist moves with the arm, so its view changes as the wrist moves.

## Exercise

Inspect the folders and identify the camera mount files, sectioned print projects, and extracted follower meshes. In your log, note which physical parts are printed and which components (servos, boards, fasteners, camera) are separate hardware.
